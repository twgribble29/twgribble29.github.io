Ty Gribble

I study how ordinary citizens think about democracy: when they defend democratic norms, when they abandon them, and why. My work combines survey and field experiments with panel data to separate stable commitments from partisan reactions to who holds power.

I graduated from the University of Texas at Austin in 2025 with a B.A. in Government (Special Honors, Phi Beta Kappa) and a minor in Philosophy. My honors thesis on Javier Milei's 2023 victory in Argentina, advised by Kurt Weyland, won the William Jennings Bryan Best Undergraduate Thesis Award. In 2026 I attended the ICPSR Summer Program as a Warren E. Miller Scholar. I am applying to PhD programs in political science for Fall 2027.
